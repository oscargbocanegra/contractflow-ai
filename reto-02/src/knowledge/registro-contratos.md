# Contract registration knowledge

The administrative analyst owns the master and policy follow-up. The mailbox is simulated by fixtures/reto-02/buzon; SharePoint is simulated under out/sharepoint. Fixtures are read-only.

RN1: Same contract ID, value, start and end is a duplicate and does not alter contractual storage. RN2: Same ID or NIT+object similarity>=0.9 with changed terms updates one row with history; an amendment inherits explicitly unchanged master fields. RN3: No match creates a row. RN4: No contract attachment or no identifiable parties/object is rejected. RN5: Confidence<0.8 blocks registration until explicit human confirmation and complete essential fields. RN6: Seed the output master from the immutable fixture. RN7: Every tool execution produces the required JSONL audit envelope.

For an unknown sender, report the unresolved commercial but do not block solely on identity. Demand values use0 with valor_indeterminado=true and human review. Missing source values remain null/confidence0. A policy threshold is not contract value. A signature month alone is insufficient for a signature day. An amendment's inherited vigente policy status is not proof of insurer-approved extension.

Alerts use an explicit hoy date and include future expiries from day0 through day60, required policies not vigente, and registrations since2026-05-30. Historical reconstruction and legal review are not automated by this challenge.

Master differences and human review are distinct: an explicit source-identified amendment may announce changed value/end dates, while contradictory identity or unannounced terms remain conflicts requiring review. A cosmetic normalized client spelling is not a new identity, but an amendment title never excuses a changed tax ID/client/country. A NIT/object-similarity match with a different contract ID must be reviewed rather than silently selecting a target. Preserve exact commercial name/region from the supplied catalog; region is catalog metadata, not a new master column.
